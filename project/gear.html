Gear for high-altitude hikes

Weather in the Andes can change in minutes. These basics will keep you warm, dry, and comfortable on the trail.

Clothing

  • A base layer that dries quickly
  • A warm middle layer, such as fleece
  • A waterproof and windproof jacket
  • A warm hat and light gloves

Footwear

  • Hiking boots or shoes with good grip, already broken in
  • Wool or synthetic socks, plus a spare pair

Sun protection

  • Sunscreen with a high SPF
  • Sunglasses and a wide-brim hat

The sun is stronger at altitude, even on cloudy days.

Essentials

  • At least two liters of water
  • Snacks with plenty of energy
  • A small first aid kit
  • A headlamp, in case the hike runs late
  • Trekking poles for steep sections

Altitude tip

Spend a couple of days in the area before a hard hike so your body can adjust. If you have health concerns, talk to a doctor before you travel.
