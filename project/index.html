Laguna Wilcacocha

A half-day hike above Huaraz with wide views of the Cordillera Blanca. A good first acclimatization walk.

Level: Easy

Laguna Churup

A steep climb of about three hours to an azure lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks.

Level: Moderate

Laguna 69

A full-day hike from Huaraz to one of the most iconic turquoise lakes in the Andes. Start early.

Level: Challenging
