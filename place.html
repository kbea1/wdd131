About Peru

Peru is a country in South America known for its rich history, ancient Inca ruins like Machu Picchu, diverse landscapes ranging from coastal deserts to the Andes mountains and the Amazon rainforest, and a vibrant culture that blends indigenous, Spanish, and other influences.
